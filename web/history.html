What the columns mean

Sunshine, sky and daylight are estimates from the light sensor, and need a calibrated sensor.

Degree days

Calculated from temperature. Heating degree days (HDD) are proportional to heating energy demand, cooling degree days (CDD) to cooling demand, and growing degree days (GDD) track plant growth. Days with gaps count only the measured part.
